# Product Identity & Positioning: TalentTrace AI
# BID Reference: Section 2.3 (Pages 5-6) & Section 10.1 (Page 27)

### 1. Proposed Product Name
TalentTrace AI

### 2. One-Line Positioning Statement
An evidence-backed interview intelligence platform that bridges automated CV screening with verifiable live conversation analysis to enable defensible, bias-free technical hiring decisions.

### 3. Product Rationale
1. Recruitment workflows frequently suffer from fragmented evaluation standards and lost conversational context, where candidate scores cannot be traced back to concrete facts.
2. TalentTrace AI anchors every automated ranking, scoring dimension, and competency evaluation directly to verifiable evidence—citing specific lines in candidate CVs and timestamped turns in the live interview transcript.
3. The name signifies total transparency and end-to-end traceability across the hiring lifecycle: from initial resume parsing to the final consolidated decision matrix.

### 4. Application Consistency
In accordance with BID Section 2.3 (Page 6), the name TalentTrace AI is applied consistently across the repository name, documentation, system headers, application UI, and presentation materials.
